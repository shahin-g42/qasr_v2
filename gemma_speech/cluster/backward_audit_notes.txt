GQASR backward audit, 2026-10-16

Bridge: acoustic forward is executed without autograd. Decoder operations still
carry gradients to the bridge, but decoder parameters are frozen.
Full: all 12 acoustic layers, convolutional frontend, output projection, 28
decoder layers and embeddings are trainable. Checkpointing and augmentation
are enabled. Bridge success does not validate these newly opened paths.

Local results (random weights, reduced width, CPU):
- 12 acoustic layers, nonzero variable-length padded features: finite gradients
  for bridge/decoder/acoustic/full modes, FP32/BF16, checkpointing on/off.
- FP32 checkpointing on/off: exactly matching losses and parameter gradients.
- All-zero features: finite loss but nonfinite acoustic gradients when unfrozen.
  Reproduced without the hybrid in upstream Gemma4AudioModel itself.
- Zero features are NOT equivalent to silent waveform log-mel features.
- These results establish a reproducible failure mode, NOT the cause of the
  real checkpoint failure. FP64 gradient-norm accumulation cannot repair it.
- Existing two-layer FP32 tests were inadequate for full-stage readiness.

Run on one free cluster GPU (no optimizer updates, clipping, DDP or saved weights):
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0 bash gemma_speech/cluster/gqasr_backward_matrix.sh --rank 0 --batches 1

Uses full config's base_model (bridge/final) and the existing SQLite index.
Each fresh process replays the same sampler anchors. Modes isolate trainability,
then checkpointing, precision and augmentation. Logs are in gemma_speech/logs.
--rank selects the original sampler rank; --update selects a zero-based update.
Use the failed rank/update when known. Matrix continues after individual failures.

For an operation-level traceback, replay a failing mode:
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0 bash gemma_speech/cluster/gqasr_stage.sh backward-audit --mode acoustic --rank 0 --update 0 --anomaly

No-update probes cannot establish optimizer or DDP stability. If failure arose
after updates, a preceding checkpoint and exact batch replay are required.
Do not change normalization epsilon, replace NaNs, or freeze layers as a claimed
root-cause fix without evidence from actual checkpoint/batch backward execution.
