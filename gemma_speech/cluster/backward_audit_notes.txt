GQASR backward audit, 2026-10-16

Bridge: acoustic forward is executed without autograd. Decoder operations still
carry gradients to the bridge, but decoder parameters are frozen.
Full: all 12 acoustic layers, convolutional frontend, output projection, 28
decoder layers and embeddings are trainable. Checkpointing and augmentation
are enabled. Bridge success does not validate these newly opened paths.

Local results (random weights, reduced width, CPU):
- 12 acoustic layers, nonzero variable-length padded features: finite gradients
  for bridge/decoder/acoustic/full modes, FP32/BF16, checkpointing on/off.
- FP32 checkpointing on/off: exactly matching losses and parameter gradients.
- All-zero features: finite loss but nonfinite acoustic gradients when unfrozen.
  Reproduced without the hybrid in upstream Gemma4AudioModel itself.
- Zero features are NOT equivalent to silent waveform log-mel features.
- These results establish a reproducible failure mode, NOT the cause of the
  real checkpoint failure. FP64 gradient-norm accumulation cannot repair it.
- Existing two-layer FP32 tests were inadequate for full-stage readiness.

Run on one free cluster GPU (no optimizer updates, clipping, DDP or saved weights):
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0 bash gemma_speech/cluster/gqasr_backward_matrix.sh --rank 0 --batches 1

Uses full config's base_model (bridge/final) and the existing SQLite index.
Each fresh process replays the same sampler anchors. Modes isolate trainability,
then checkpointing, precision and augmentation. Logs are in gemma_speech/logs.
--rank selects the original sampler rank; --update selects a zero-based update.
Use the failed rank/update when known. Matrix continues after individual failures.

For an operation-level traceback, replay a failing mode:
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0 bash gemma_speech/cluster/gqasr_stage.sh backward-audit --mode acoustic --rank 0 --update 0 --anomaly

No-update probes cannot establish optimizer or DDP stability. If failure arose
after updates, a preceding checkpoint and exact batch replay are required.
Do not change normalization epsilon, replace NaNs, or freeze layers as a claimed
root-cause fix without evidence from actual checkpoint/batch backward execution.

REAL CHECKPOINT AUDIT: 2026-10-16, node inception-H100-hpc-051
Model: outputs/bridge/final, original full-stage config, batch 20, update 0.
Torch 2.11.0+cu128, CUDA runtime 12.8; no optimizer steps or weight writes.
Code revision for multi-rank replay: 2684f14.

27 distinct first-update rank batches checked with full BF16 backward:
- Passed: 0-11, 24-25, 48-53, 72-74 (23 batches).
- Failed: 12, 26, 54, 75 (4 batches). Each replay stopped at its first failure.
- Failures were genuine nonfinite acoustic parameter gradients; bridge and
  decoder gradients were finite. No DDP or gradient clipping was involved.
- Rank 12 loss remained finite: 3.5793547630.

Controls on rank 12:
- Frozen encoder, train decoder+bridge, original augmentation: PASS.
- Full unfrozen model, no augmentation: PASS.
- Full unfrozen model, FP32, original augmentation: FAIL in acoustic gradients.
- Autograd anomaly: MmBackward0 in acoustic lconv1d.linear_end returned NaN.
- Full no-checkpointing at batch 20: OOM on dedicated 80GB H100, inconclusive.

Controls on ALL FOUR failing batches (12, 26, 54, 75):
- Only SpecAugment disabled, waveform augmentation and code switching retained:
  PASS on all four.
- Same original SpecAugment mask locations, replace newly zeroed feature cells
  with valid-frame per-utterance/per-frequency means: PASS on all four.
  This was a process-local experimental override, NOT a production code change.
  Mean-fill losses respectively: 3.5597003, 3.5994594, 3.5135832, 3.4584839.
  These losses do not establish speech quality.

Conclusion: zero-filled SpecAugment regions are the isolated trigger for the
reproduced real-batch acoustic backward failures. The copied QASR augmentation
was not validated adequately against this acoustic encoder. FP64 norm clipping
cannot fix this. The streaming dataset was not in the tested/pushed code.
Frozen bridge training did not backpropagate through the acoustic encoder and
also did not enable augmentation. Neither architecture incompatibility in
principle nor an intrinsically corrupt checkpoint follows from these results.

Limitations: backward-only replay at initial weights; not a completed optimizer
or distributed training validation. Mean fill needs a proper implementation,
regression coverage and wider stress tests before use in production. Current
training configs, model math and saved checkpoints have NOT been changed.

Logs on cluster: gemma_speech/logs/gqasr-backward-audit-20261016T*.log.
The initial no-checkpointing tests on GPU0 overlapped a user-launched audit and
OOMed; those results were discarded. Our later replays used separate GPUs.
